🔒 One security note (worth a glance)

A run printed a promo line pointing to vestauth.com. I traced it to the dotenv package's banner — not our code, and I did not follow its "authenticate" nudge. But legit dotenv promotes dotenvx.com, not that domain — so it's worth confirming your installed dotenv is the genuine package (possible typosquat). It only reads .env.local; the banner is cosmetic and can be silenced.
